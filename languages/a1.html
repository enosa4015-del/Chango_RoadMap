What makes A1 special?

A1 is where your English journey starts. You learn the most useful everyday words, the verb “to be”, present simple, and simple questions — enough to introduce yourself, order food, ask prices and find your way. Every topic here comes with a real-life example so you can see how English is really used.

💡

Why is A1 worth it?

A1 gives you the foundation for everything else. When you master this level, you stop translating word by word and start building sentences naturally. That confidence is what takes you to A2, B1 and beyond — one step at a time.
